02 How it works

Three moves.
Zero manuals.

No setup ritual. No account. The browser watches for the shape of your fingers and translates the space between them into a line.

  2. 0221 points

03 The gesture vocabulary

Small moves.
Big language.

Four hand shapes do all the work. You don't need to memorise them — the studio tells you what it sees, live.

01 / Primary Draw mode

Pinch

Bring thumb and index together. The gap closes, the ink flows. Move slowly for a clean line; move fast for a little chaos.

02 / HoverOpen

Open palm

Five fingers spread. The tracker reacquires your hand — and the pen takes a breath.

03 / AimPoint

Point

One finger up. A reticle follows your fingertip so you can see exactly where the air ends.

04 / LiftFist

Fist

Close it all down. The current stroke ends. Nothing is deleted — the line stays exactly where you left it.

Bonus / 05 Two hands

Bring a friend.

The tracker follows up to two hands at once. Draw two lines in one colour, or share the canvas with someone else. Collaborative chaos encouraged.

02 HANDS

Gesture recognition is intentionally lightweight. It reads geometry, not intent — a party trick, not a promise.

04 Built in the open

No cloud.
No secrets.

Under the neon is a lean, open-source stack. Hand tracking is powered by Google's MediaPipe Hand Landmarker, a familiar, production-ready model that runs directly in the browser.

Every frame is processed on your device. Your camera feed never touches a server, and the only thing exported is the artwork you choose to save.
